Questions

Frequently asked

What makes Visual SNAP different from a traditional backtester?

You begin with the chart, not a blank strategy form. Click a candle to capture your setup, configure the RSI and exit rules, then run a historical backtest.

Does Backtest Mechanic place live orders?

No. Backtest Mechanic is a research and historical strategy-testing application, not a live order execution bot.

Can I save an unfinished strategy?

Yes. Original and edited JSON can be saved independently, even before validation. Validation is required before running a backtest.

What happens when validation fails?

Review the validation feedback, edit your strategy JSON, and validate again before backtesting.

Does it run in the cloud?

No. The Basic application runs locally on Windows. AI-assisted features are planned for higher editions.

Which indicators are included in the free beta?

The initial Visual SNAP beta focuses on RSI-based historical matching. MACD and moving-average matching are planned for later development.
